Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. In a bowl, mash the bananas until smooth.
  3. Stir in the oats until fully combined and a thick dough forms.
  4. If desired, fold in chocolate chips or raisins.
  5. Scoop spoonfuls of the mixture onto the baking sheet and flatten slightly (they won’t spread much).
  6. Bake for 12–15 minutes, or until the cookies are set and lightly golden.
  7. Let cool for a few minutes, then enjoy!
