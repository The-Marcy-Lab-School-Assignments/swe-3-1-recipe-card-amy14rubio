Ingredients (Serves 4)

  • Pasta — (I prefer Farfalle) - 1 box
  • Garlic — 2-3 cloves minced
  • Andouille Sausage — 4 sausages, cut into half moons
  • Cherry Tomatoes — 1 pint quartered
  • Baby Spinach — 8-10oz
